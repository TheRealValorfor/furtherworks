# Furtherworks

**GitHub:** [TheRealValorfor/furtherworks](https://github.com/TheRealValorfor/furtherworks)

Worlds Further endgame catalyst. Prove you finished Relics, Aeronautics, Ars, dragons, factory, and the feast table, then spend **The Further** on Create's creative power items.

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1 • NeoForge • Create 6

**Optional (recipes gated):** Relics • Create Aeronautics + Simulated • Ars Nouveau • Ice and Fire • Farmer's Delight • Pam's HarvestCraft 2 Food Extended

## Loop

1. Mechanical-craft four **hearts** (Relic, Sky, Source, Homestead).
2. Mechanical-craft **The Further** from those hearts plus all three dragonsteels, a netherite block, and a blaze cake.
3. Consume **one Further + one mundane item** per creative unlock.

| Spend | Get |
|---|---|
| Hand Crank | Creative Motor |
| Fluid Tank | Creative Fluid Tank |
| Blaze Cake | Creative Blaze Cake |
| Engine Assembly | Creative Physics Staff |

**Not craftable:** Handheld Worldshaper, Creative Crate.

A second motor means a second Further.

## Relic Heart

One of each Relics unique that exists in Relics 0.10 (28 items) in a 4×7 mechanical crafter. Broken relics stay for Relicworks milling. More Relics addon items are not required.

## Build

```bash
./gradlew build
```

Jar: `build/libs/furtherworks-1.0.2.jar`
